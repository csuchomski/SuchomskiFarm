Before you ask

Do I need to draw my paddocks to start?

No. Import the fence lines you already have as KML, or type paddock names and acres and add shapes later. The record works either way.

Does it work at the gate, with no signal?

[ANSWER THIS HONESTLY — offline behaviour is the first question every grazier asks, and the answer belongs here rather than in a support email.]

Can I get my data out?

Yes — the records print, and the ledger and herd export. It's your farm's history; it should never be hostage to a subscription.

Will it do my taxes?

It gets your Schedule F to the line, with cattle depreciation worked out, so your preparer starts from a schedule instead of a shoebox. It is not a tax filing service and it does not give tax advice.
